Chart 2: Where – Proportional-symbol store map (Web Mercator projection) with ranking

Purpose

Show where revenue is generated across the three stores.

Functionality

  • Circle area is proportional to revenue
  • Click a store on the map or ranking to filter the page; click again to release

Insights

  • Hell's Kitchen leads: $236,511 (50,735 transactions)
  • Astoria $232,244; Lower Manhattan $230,057
  • Spread is only $6,454 (2.8%): the stores perform very evenly

Design reflection

Area (square-root scale), not radius, avoids exaggerating differences, and the spread is stated beside the map. The basemap is a disclosed simplification.
