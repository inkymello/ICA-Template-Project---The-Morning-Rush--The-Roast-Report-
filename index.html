Group 4: The Roast Report: The Morning Rush

An interactive D3.js visualisation of point-of-sale data from three New York coffee shops (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen and Lower Manhattan, January–June 2023). Our overarching question: How do sales patterns across the three stores vary by time, location and volume, and what do they imply for staffing, stock and site performance?

Each team member built one chart; all three share one filter set (Store / Category / Month).
