Chart 1: When – Day × hour matrix heat map (7 days × 15 hours)

Purpose

Reveal when transaction activity is highest and lowest across the week.

Functionality

  • Hover an hour to highlight it across the week
  • Click to pin a selection
  • Reacts to all shared filters

Insights

  • 7am–11am carries 45% of all transactions
  • Busiest hour is 10am (18,545); peak cell is Tuesday 10am (2,846)
  • Same pattern every day; evenings are very quiet

Design reflection

A matrix was chosen over seven line charts so both time dimensions compare at a glance. Exact counts are printed in each cell so colour is not the only cue.
